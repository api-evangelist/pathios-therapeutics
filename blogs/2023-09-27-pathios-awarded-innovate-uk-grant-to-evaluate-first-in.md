Today, we have announced that the company has been awarded a £567K (~US$727K) grant from Innovate UK, the UK government’s innovation agency. The grant funding will enable the company to expand its development of novel small molecule GPR65 inhibitors into the area of a possible treatment of malignant brain tumors.
